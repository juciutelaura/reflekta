# Reflekta

## 1. Product Vision

Reflekta is an AI-guided self-reflection experience that helps people explore their thoughts, questions, fears, beliefs, values, and recurring patterns.

Reflekta is inspired by the traditional Indian Leela/Lila self-knowledge game, but it is intentionally designed as a secular, modern digital experience.

The product combines:

* a structured game-like journey,
* reflective wisdom cards,
* personal reflection,
* and AI-guided conversation.

The core idea is simple:

> **The card provides the stimulus. The AI facilitates reflection. The user creates the meaning.**

Reflekta does not tell people who they are or what their lives mean. It helps them look at their own thoughts from different perspectives.

### Product tagline

> **See your thoughts differently.**

---

## 2. The Problem

People often have questions that are difficult to think through alone.

These questions may relate to:

* relationships,
* work and ambition,
* fear,
* uncertainty,
* identity,
* change,
* control,
* attachment,
* values,
* decisions,
* recurring emotional or behavioral patterns,
* or simply a feeling that something in their life needs closer examination.

Traditional journaling can help, but many people struggle to know what to write or what questions to ask themselves next.

Conversational AI can provide an interactive reflection partner, but generic AI conversations can easily become:

* overly interpretive,
* advice-driven,
* repetitive,
* psychologically presumptuous,
* or disconnected from a meaningful structure.

Reflekta provides that structure.

The game introduces an unexpected reflection stimulus, while AI helps the user explore their own response to it.

---

## 3. Target User

Reflekta is intended for people who:

* enjoy self-reflection;
* have a question or intention they want to explore;
* are curious about their own thoughts and patterns;
* enjoy journaling or reflective exercises;
* want an interactive alternative to traditional journaling;
* are open to exploring perspectives without expecting a definitive answer.

Reflekta is not designed specifically for people seeking clinical mental-health treatment, diagnosis, or crisis support.

---

## 4. Core User Experience

A Reflekta journey begins with a question, intention, or topic the user wants to explore.

The user then enters a game-like journey.

The basic experience is:

1. The user creates an intention.
2. Reflekta may help clarify the intention.
3. The user starts a journey.
4. The user rolls a dice.
5. The game deterministically selects the corresponding card.
6. The user reads the wisdom card.
7. The user reflects on the card.
8. AI facilitates a conversation around the user's reflection.
9. The user continues the journey with another card.
10. At the end, Reflekta provides a summary of the journey.
11. The journey becomes part of the user's history and may contribute to longer-term reflection.

The AI does not decide which card the user receives based on the user's intention.

Randomness is an intentional part of the experience.

---

## 5. The Role of the Card

Cards are the central reflective stimulus of Reflekta.

Each card contains author-created wisdom/reflection text.

Cards may explore themes such as:

* Compassion
* Fear
* Control
* Attachment
* Change
* Acceptance
* Ambition
* Uncertainty
* Identity
* and other aspects of human experience.

The meaning of a card is not fixed for the user.

The same card may evoke completely different thoughts in different people, or even different thoughts for the same person at different moments.

Therefore:

> **A card is a reflection stimulus, not a prediction or an answer.**

Card content is static and authored. The LLM does not generate or redefine the card's core wisdom text during gameplay.

---

## 6. The Role of AI

AI is the facilitator of the reflection process.

Its role is to:

* ask thoughtful questions;
* help the user explore their own response;
* encourage deeper reflection;
* notice relevant context from the conversation;
* help the user consider alternative perspectives;
* summarize what emerged during a session;
* potentially identify recurring themes across journeys.

The AI should behave more like a thoughtful reflection partner than an authority.

The AI helps the user think.

It does not think on behalf of the user.

### Core principle

> **AI helps you think, not tells you what to think.**

Another core principle:

> **AI does not tell you who you are. It helps you explore who you might be.**

---

## 7. AI Conversation Philosophy

The AI should begin with open, non-leading questions.

Examples:

* "What came to mind when you read this card?"
* "Which part of the card stood out to you?"
* "What was your first thought when you read it?"

The AI should not immediately assume that the card is connected to the user's original intention.

For example, it should avoid immediately asking:

> "How does this card relate to your relationship problem?"

Instead, it should first allow the user to determine whether a connection exists.

The conversation should:

* ask one meaningful question at a time;
* start broadly;
* follow the user's response;
* gradually deepen when appropriate;
* allow disagreement;
* allow the user to reject an interpretation;
* avoid forcing meaning or connections.

If the user does not see a connection between the card and their situation, the AI should accept that rather than trying to manufacture one.

---

## 8. What Reflekta Is Not

Reflekta is not:

* a fortune-telling system;
* a prediction system;
* an oracle;
* a source of divine messages;
* an astrology system;
* a karma or destiny system;
* a chakra or mystical energy system;
* a system that claims cards have supernatural powers;
* a diagnostic system;
* a replacement for a mental-health professional;
* a system that tells users what decisions they should make.

Reflekta should not present random events as supernatural signs or predetermined messages.

The product may be inspired by Leela, but it should not require the user to believe in its spiritual or metaphysical interpretation.

---

## 9. User Agency

The user owns the meaning of their experience.

Reflekta should encourage curiosity rather than certainty.

The system should use tentative language when discussing possible interpretations.

For example:

* "Could this be connected to...?"
* "I wonder whether..."
* "Does that resonate with you?"
* "One possible perspective is..."

Rather than:

* "This card means that you..."
* "You are afraid of..."
* "Your problem is..."
* "The universe is telling you..."

The user must always be able to disagree with the AI.

---

## 10. Reflection Over Advice

Reflekta prioritizes reflection before advice.

The product should not immediately turn a user's reflection into a list of recommendations or life instructions.

For example, if a user says:

> "I feel stuck in my career."

The initial response should help the user explore the thought rather than immediately producing a career action plan.

The goal is to help the user understand their own thinking before deciding what to do.

---

## 11. The Long-Term Journey

A single Reflekta session can provide a useful reflection.

The larger product opportunity is the journey across multiple sessions.

Over time, Reflekta may help users notice:

* recurring themes;
* recurring fears;
* repeated questions;
* values that appear frequently;
* changes in perspective;
* questions that remain unresolved;
* patterns across different journeys;
* areas where the user's thinking has changed.

The purpose of long-term memory is not to build a psychological profile that claims to define the user.

It is to help the user notice patterns in their own reflections.

---

## 12. Product Principles

### 12.1 Reflection over prediction

Reflekta creates space for reflection rather than predicting outcomes.

### 12.2 User meaning over AI interpretation

The user decides what a card means to them.

### 12.3 Curiosity over certainty

The product should encourage exploration rather than claim definitive answers.

### 12.4 Facilitation over advice

AI should guide thinking before attempting to provide solutions.

### 12.5 Randomness matters

The card should not be selected by AI according to what it thinks the user needs.

### 12.6 Wisdom lives in the card

The core wisdom content is authored and intentionally designed.

AI facilitates the user's interaction with that wisdom.

### 12.7 Simplicity over unnecessary complexity

The simplest system that provides a meaningful user experience should be preferred.

Technical complexity should be introduced only when a real product requirement justifies it.

### 12.8 The user remains in control

The user can disagree with the AI, reject interpretations, or decide that a card is not relevant.

---

## 13. Product Differentiation

Reflekta is not simply an AI chatbot.

The core experience comes from the interaction of:

**Intention → Random stimulus → Reflection → AI facilitation → New perspective**

The structured game creates an element of surprise and prevents the AI from simply confirming what the user already believes.

The AI then provides conversational depth around the user's own response.

The combination creates a different experience from both:

* traditional journaling, where the user has to generate the structure themselves;
* generic AI chat, where the AI often provides answers rather than facilitating reflection.

---

## 14. MVP Product Boundary

The first version of Reflekta should validate the fundamental experience.

The MVP should allow a user to:

* create an account;
* create an intention;
* optionally clarify the intention with AI;
* start a journey;
* roll the dice;
* receive a deterministic card;
* read the card;
* write a reflection;
* have an AI-guided reflection conversation;
* continue to another card;
* complete a session;
* receive a session summary;
* view basic journey history.

The MVP should focus on proving that this core experience is useful and engaging.

The following should not be required for the initial MVP:

* native mobile applications;
* social networking;
* psychologist dashboards;
* complex billing systems;
* large multi-agent architectures;
* unnecessary external integrations;
* complex infrastructure;
* advanced personalization before the core experience is validated.

---

## 15. Success Criteria

The first product question is not:

> "Can we build sophisticated AI?"

The first question is:

> **"Does this experience genuinely help people reflect?"**

Early success should therefore be evaluated through user behavior and feedback such as:

* Do users complete journeys?
* Do users want to continue to another card?
* Do users find the AI conversation useful?
* Do users feel that the AI understood their reflection without imposing meaning?
* Do users discover perspectives they had not considered?
* Do users return for another journey?
* Do users perceive value in looking back at previous reflections?

Technical sophistication is secondary to the quality of the experience.

---

## 16. Product North Star

Reflekta should leave the user with:

> **A different way of seeing their own thoughts.**

Not an answer.

Not a prediction.

Not a diagnosis.

Not a prescription.

A new perspective.
